Server

A cozy pixel card-hunting RPG. Fight with cards and spells, team up with up to three friends, then wind down on your farm.

Play free in your browser Get the Android app (32 MB)

Works on PC, tablets and phones (turn the phone sideways) · Your hero is saved on your own device

What you can do

Hunt monsters for their cards, build a deck that fits how you fight, and grow from a nobody into a legend at your own pace.

Collect and build

Every monster drops cards. Build decks, fuse them, find combos and level up skills that change how you fight.

Co-op with friends

Up to four heroes in one world. More players means tougher monsters. Join with a 4-letter code.

Black Holes and raids

Rifts tear open in the fields. Step inside for bosses, take on raids, climb the tower and test yourself in the arena.

Summon

Spin the crystal machine for rare cards with coins you earn by playing. No real money, ever.

A home to come back to

Grow crops, raise animals, cook meals and decorate. A calm place to end the night.

Slow moments

Fishing, campfires, a diary and a photo album. Cozy mode keeps everything gentle when you want to relax.

Play on your phone

Play right in your phone's browser with no install (choose "Add to Home Screen" in the browser menu to keep it like an app), or get the Android app.

1

On your Android phone, tap Get the Android app above to download it.

2

Open the downloaded file. Android asks to allow installs from your browser: allow it this once.

3

Tap Install, then open Zenonia Card Hunters. It plays sideways, full screen.

The app isn't on the Play Store, so Android shows a warning: that's normal for apps installed from a website. Your hero in the app is separate from the browser one; use Send Hero in Settings to move it across.
